Statutory Norms

Facilities & Safety Provisions Across Employers

Common baseline standards across public sector banking, central government, and regulatory bodies.

Maternity & Childcare Leave

Standard 180 days paid maternity leave under Maternity Benefit Act (2017). Child Care Leave (CCL) provided in Central Govt, RBI, and NABARD cadres.

POSH Compliance & Grievance

Mandatory Internal Complaints Committees (ICC) under the POSH Act, 2013 across every regional, administrative, and circle office.

Fee Waivers & Horizontal Reservation

Application fees waived or heavily subsidised for women in exams like SSC CGL and select PSU drives.

Urban vs Rural Posting Safety

RBI, LIC, and NABARD regional offices are primarily located in state capitals/major metros with dedicated staff quarters and secure transit support.

Medical Cover & Welfare Loans

PSU health schemes, group accidental coverage, and concessional staff loan perks provided across public financial institutions.

Transfer Preferences

Progressive spouse-ground posting rules and prioritized urban postings where feasible in large networks like SBI.
